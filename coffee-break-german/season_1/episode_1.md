# Coffee Break German - Season 1 Episode 1 Notes

## Basic Phrases and Greetings

| German | English |
|--------|---------|
| Hallo | Hello |
| Auf Wiedersehen | Goodbye (formal) |
| Tschüss | Goodbye (informal) |
| Bis morgen | Until tomorrow |
| Guten Morgen | Good morning |

## Questions and Responses

| German | English |
|--------|---------|
| Bist du fertig? | Are you ready? |
| Lasst uns anfangen | Let's get started |
| Wie geht's? | How is it going? |

| German | English |
|--------|---------|
| Mir geht's gut | I am good / It's going good for me |
| Mir geht's sehr gut | I am very fine |
| Mir geht's nicht so gut | I'm not doing so well |
| Mir geht's wunderbar | I feel wonderful |
| Mir geht's ausgezeichnet | I'm feeling excellent |
